# Support

For product help, use [Tescam's website](https://teslacam.eu/) or email [contact+teslacam@magrathean.uk](mailto:contact+teslacam@magrathean.uk), the support address published there. See the [privacy notice](https://teslacam.eu/privacy/) before sending diagnostic material.

## Useful report details

Include the app version and build or source commit, device and operating system, whether you used the native app or CLI, and the steps that produced the problem. Describe the expected and actual result. For export problems, include the preset, selected cameras and range, and a redacted error message.

Use synthetic or anonymised examples where possible. Logs, manifests, paths, screenshots and footage can reveal locations, timestamps, number plates and other personal information. Do not send original footage unless it is needed and you are authorised to share it.

For authorised source development, see [RUNBOOK.md](RUNBOOK.md). For licensing questions, see [license.md](license.md). Report vulnerabilities privately through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
